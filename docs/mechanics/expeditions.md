# Expeditions

## Tarot Card System
Expeditions are built around a tarot card mechanic that provides variety and mystical theming appropriate for a forest cat colony.

## Core Mechanics
- **Daily Energy System**: Players have limited expedition plays per day
- **Deck-Based**: Players use a tarot deck to generate expedition scenarios
- **Spread Selection**: Players choose different tarot spreads for their expeditions
- **Card-to-Minigame**: Each drawn card corresponds to a specific minigame scenario

## Card Mechanics
- **Upright vs Reversed**: Card orientation affects the type and difficulty of minigames
- **Thematic Integration**: Card meanings translate directly to gameplay scenarios
  - Example: "The Fool" (upright) = finding a new cat in the wild
  - Example: "The Fool" (reversed) = encountering a hostile cat that wants to fight

## Cat Integration
- **Active Participation**: Players select which cats to bring on expeditions
- **Trait Influence**: Cat traits and abilities affect minigame outcomes and expedition success
- **Character Development**: Expeditions serve as opportunities for cat progression and growth

## Expedition Flow
1. Player selects cats for the expedition party
2. Player chooses a tarot spread layout
3. Cards are drawn and revealed according to the spread
4. Each card triggers its corresponding minigame
5. Cat traits influence minigame mechanics and outcomes
6. Expedition concludes with rewards based on performance
