# Camp

## Overview
The camp serves as the main hub where players view and manage their cat colony between expeditions.

## Core MVP Features
- **Visual Camp Display**: Single screen showing the entire camp area including dens, open areas, and environmental details
- **Cat Visibility**: All cats in the colony are visible simultaneously within the camp view
- **Cat Interaction**: Players can tap on any individual cat to open their stats, info, and management menus

## Camp Layout
- Dens and shelter areas where cats rest
- Open areas where cats can roam and interact
- Environmental elements that create atmosphere and context for the forest colony

## Cat Management Interface
- Tapping any cat opens their individual information panel
- Access to cat stats, traits, customization options, and other management features
- Primary method for players to interact with and learn about their individual cats
