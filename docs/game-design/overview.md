# Cat Colony Game - Overview

## Game Description

### Overview
A cat management game where players manage a colony of wild cats living in a forest. The game combines collection mechanics with progression systems, centered around unique, customizable cats that serve as both companions and gameplay tools.

### Core Concept
- **Collection-Driven Progression**: Players grow their colony by acquiring new cats, each with unique traits and appearances
- **Customization Focus**: Cat customization is a major engagement driver, allowing players to design cats according to their preferences
- **Social Elements**: Players can trade cats and items through marketplace systems
- **Game as a Service Model**: Ongoing content and engagement without traditional win conditions

### Primary Gameplay Loop
1. **Tarot Expeditions** - Use daily energy to embark on tarot-based adventures
2. **Cat Selection** - Choose which cats to bring, affecting expedition outcomes
3. **Collection & Progression** - Gain new cats, resources, and strengthen existing cats
4. **Colony Management** - Manage and customize cats in the central camp area
5. **Social Trading** - Trade with other players to expand collection

### Key Features
- Unique cat traits affecting gameplay (appearance, age, skills, behavioral characteristics)
- Player-to-player trading and marketplace systems
